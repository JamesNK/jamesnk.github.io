The industrious people over at Mindscape have [released version 1.1](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/blog/?p=58) of their LightSpeed domain modeling/ORM framework. I used LightSpeed while it was in beta with my [first Silverlight application](/archive/2007/07/03/first-silverlight-project-trafficwarden) and it has definitely matured a lot since then.
 
There are a bunch of [new features](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/Products/LightSpeed/changelog.aspx) in the 1.1 release including my pet feature request: default ordering of entities and child entity collections 😊 Now if you have a Purchase entity, the PurchaseLine collection on it can be explicitly ordered by line number for you when it loads. Cool!
 
You can find out more about LightSpeed [here](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/products/LightSpeed/).
