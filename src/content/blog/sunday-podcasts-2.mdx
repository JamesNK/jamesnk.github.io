This American Life** - [Classifieds](http://www.thislife.org/Radio_Episode.aspx?sched=1299)

[This American Life](http://www.thislife.org) is a fantastic weekly podcast about everyday aspects of life. Each week’s show has a theme and then a number of stories around that theme.

This episode is based on stories gathered from classified ads, all found from a single single newspaper on a single day. A man who posted an ad about a missing dog, a personnel ad to win back a lost love, and my favourite: assembling a disparate group of musicians from classified ads to form a band for one day only and record a song - Rocket Man.

[Jon Langford and the One Day Band - Rocket Man.mp3](http://james.newtonking.com/downloads/music/Jon%20Langford%20and%20the%20One%20Day%20Band%20-%20Rocket%20Man.mp3):

**Hanselminutes** - [Visiting Fog Creek Software and Joel Spolsky](http://www.hanselminutes.com/default.aspx?showID=176)

Scott Hanselman interviews Joel Spolsky about technology, business, blogging and shark jumping. I can’t say I agree with many of Spolsky’s opinions on programming ([eww](http://www.joelonsoftware.com/items/2003/10/13.html)) but he has a good head for business and marketing.

![I hope this has taught you kids a lesson: kids never learn.](http://james.newtonking.com/images/SundayPodcasts2_F305/aardvark_thumb.png "I hope this has taught you kids a lesson: kids never learn.")

While Fog Creek is most known for a project management tool called Fogbuz they also have created a remote desktop service called Copilot. A DVD I brought not long after I started my first software development job is a documentary about Copilot called [**Aardvark'd: Twelve Weeks with Geeks**](http://www.projectaardvark.com/movie/). Aardvark is a fun little movie about the software and the small group of interns who made it. Recommended.
