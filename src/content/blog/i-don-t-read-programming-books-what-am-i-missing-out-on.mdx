Of Jeff Atwood's list of 16 [recommended books for developers](http://www.codinghorror.com/blog/archives/000020.html) I have read zero. Ditto this list of [Top 20 Programming Books](http://yaneshtyagi.blogspot.com/2008/04/top-20-programming-books.html). Searching though Amazon recommended programming book lists also yielded no hits.
 
When learning a new software development technology or idea I have always turned to the web. Blogs, articles, open source projects and so on, leaving the books I read largely to fiction.
 
What am I missing out on? Are blogs and other online content enough or will reading [The Pragmatic Programming](http://www.amazon.com/Pragmatic-Programmer-Journeyman-Master/dp/020161622X/) (common to almost every list) make me a better software developer?
