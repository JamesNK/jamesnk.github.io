**![Ahoy hoy?](http://james.newtonking.com/images/SundayPodcasts_F961/podcast_logo.jpg "Ahoy hoy?") Hanselminutes** - [Mo Interviews Scott Hanselman](http://www.hanselminutes.com/default.aspx?showID=171)
  
One of my highlights of TechEd NZ 2008 was meeting [Scott Hanselman](http://www.hanselman.com/blog/). Chatting about random techie things in the speakers room and working side by side to prepare our presentations was a lot of fun. Here Mo, Scott’s wife, interviews him about what it is like being The HanselMan.
  
**Econtalk** - [Wales on Wikipedia](http://www.econtalk.org/archives/2009/03/wales_on_wikipe.html)
  
Wikipedia is a fantastic example of harnessing the cloud to collaboratively create something amazing. [Jimmy Wales](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Wales) talks about what has made Wikipedia successful, dealing with conflict and how Wikipedia is a self managing entity.
  
**Dungeons & Dragons Podcast** - [Penny Arcade Episode 1](http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/drfe/20080530)
  
The [Penny Arcade](http://www.penny-arcade.com/) guys are always funny and listening to them play D&D is hilarious. The first of episode of [many](http://www.wizards.com/dnd/rsspodcast.xml).
