When I [moved my blog](http://james.newtonking.com/archive/2007/08/28/change-of-location-http-james-newtonking-com.aspx) what seems like a while ago now, [Technorati](http://technorati.com/) indexing broke. Today I finally decided to do something about it.
 
After reading the FAQs and making sure it wasn't a problem here I emailed Technorati. Less than 20 minutes later they had read my email, fixed the issue, re-indexed this blog and sent a reply.
 
Good job Technorati!
