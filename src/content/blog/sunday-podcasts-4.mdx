**Penny Arcade TV** - [Ping Pong 1](http://www.penny-arcade.com/patv/pa-the-series/109/)/[Ping Pong 2](http://www.penny-arcade.com/patv/pa-the-series/110/)
  
The guys at Penny Arcade have branched out from podcasts to a [web TV show](http://www.penny-arcade.com/patv/) with predictably hilarious results.
  
There are a lot of good episodes ([PA's crazy hire process!](http://www.penny-arcade.com/patv/pa-the-series/112/)) but I really love this duo covering Penny Arcade's obsession with Ping Pong and a rematch game against Bungie, the makers of my [beloved Halo](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1sALCVfWoyw).
  
Smack talk, slow-mo ping pong action shots and an agonist sporting a sinister English accent - what's not to like?

**NPR: Planet Money Podcast** - [We Bought A Toxic Asset!](http://www.npr.org/blogs/money/2010/03/podcast_we_bought_a_toxic_asse.html)
  
I wouldn't call [Planet Money](http://www.npr.org/blogs/money/) a pure economics podcast - it definitely has a bit of an opinion about some economic social issues so viewer discretion is advised - but that doesn't mean it isn't interesting.
  
This episode the people at Planet Money buy a toxic asset and explain in simple language what a toxic asset is and why and how you would buy one. It is a lot more entertaining than I ever would have thought possible.
  
Oh and bonus points for playing both [The Gambler](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kn481KcjvMo) and [Danger Zone](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V8rZWw9HE7o) in a single podcast!
