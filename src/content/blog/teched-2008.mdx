Tomorrow I fly up to [TechEd NZ 2008](http://www.microsoft.com/nz/teched08/index.aspx). Intergen is running the hands on labs again so expect to see groups of people in [striking yellow camo pants](/Media/Default/images/blog/clipboard.png).

![Weaseling out of things is important to learn. It's what separates us from the animals ... except the weasel.](../../assets/blog/teched-2008/teched08.png "Weaseling out of things is important to learn. It's what separates us from the animals ... except the weasel.")

As always Intergenites are also presenting a number of sessions:

- SOA205 - Extending the Application Platform with Cloud Services - ***Chris Auld***
- SOA209 - The Road to "Oslo" : The Microsoft Services and Modeling Platform - ***Chris Auld***
- OFC301 - Creating Content Centric Publishing Sites with MOSS 2007 - ***Mark Orange**, Zac Smith*
- OFC305 - Best Practice ECM with SharePoint 2007 - *Michael Noel, **Mark Orange***
- OFC404 - Open XML Development Turning SharePoint Data into Microsoft Office Documents - ***James Newton-King**, Reed Shaffner*

Yup I am presenting at TechEd! I'm not sure which I am more of: excited or nervous. [Good times](http://hestia.typepad.com/flatlander/2008/08/official-teched.html).
