The guys at [Mindscape](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/) have released another WPF product call [WPF Elements](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/products/WpfElements/default.aspx). This time it is a set of WPF controls aimed primarily at line of business apps: masked textboxes, numeric textboxes, date time pickers, a multi-column treeview, and more.
 
I have used a product of theirs call [Lightspeed](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/products/LightSpeed/default.aspx) on a number of projects and Mindscape has always been really prompt when responding to queries. If you are developing business WPF applications then Elements is well worth checking out.
