![I've sold monorails to Brockway, Ogdenville, and North Haverbrook, and by gum, it put them on the map!](http://james.newtonking.com/Media/Default/images/blog/mr_rawlogo.gif "I've sold monorails to Brockway, Ogdenville, and North Haverbrook, and by gum, it put them on the map!")I learnt today that [MonoRail](http://www.castleproject.org/monorail/index.html) is using [Json.NET](http://james.newtonking.com/projects/json-net.aspx) for its JSON support. The license I release my free projects under is pretty permissive so I don't often get a chance to learn what they're being used with. To hear that Json.NET is being used with something the size and quality of MonoRail is pretty cool 😊

Thanks to [JD](http://blog.bluecog.co.nz/) and the guys at [Mindscape](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/) for the heads up.
