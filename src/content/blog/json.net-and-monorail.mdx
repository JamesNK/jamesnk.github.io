I learnt today that [MonoRail](http://www.castleproject.org/monorail/index.html) is using [Json.NET](http://www.newtonsoft.com/json) for its JSON support. The license I release my free projects under is pretty permissive so I don't often get a chance to learn what they're being used with. To hear that Json.NET is being used with something the size and quality of MonoRail is pretty cool 😊

Thanks to [JD](http://blog.bluecog.co.nz/) and the guys at [Mindscape](http://www.mindscape.co.nz/) for the heads up.
