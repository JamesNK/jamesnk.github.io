I have been [memed](http://andrewpeters.net/2008/07/11/when-memes-attack/).  

**How old were you when you started programming?**  

19.  

**How did you get started in programming?**  

Most of my experience with computers as a teenager was playing [computer games](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-strike). It wasn't until my second year of doing commerce at university that I did a [programming paper](http://www.victoria.ac.nz/fca/Course%20Outlines/2003/Trimester%201/INFO/INFO215.pdf) and really got into it. Oddly I have never had the desire to write a computer game.  

**What was your first language?**  

Visual Basic 6.  

**What was the first real program you wrote?**  

Some lameo VB6/Access data entry form for university.  

**What languages have you used since you started programming?**  

Visual Basic, SQL, PL/SQL, JavaScript, C#, Python, Delphi.  

**What was your first professional programming gig?**  

Building a transaction management system for a small Internet startup.  

**If you knew then what you know now, would you have started programming?**  

Yes.  

**If there is one thing you learned along the way that you would tell new developers, what would it be?**  

The software development world is a huge place. Don't be afraid to spend a couple of years exploring and finding what you’re most passionate about.  

**What’s the most fun you’ve ever had… programming?**  

Solving challenging business and technology problems in a simple, maintainable way that hides all the complexity behind a easy to use interface. [Json.NET](http://www.newtonsoft.com/json) and and the [RuntimePageOptimizer](http://runtimepageoptimiser.com/) are two projects that I have really enjoyed working on.
