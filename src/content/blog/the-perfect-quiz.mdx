Forget personality, IQ, love and every other test or quiz on the internet; the [Web 2.0 or Star Wars Character](http://www.cerado.com/web20quiz.htm) test is here. The creator has somehow managed to combine the ridiculing of the silly Web 2.0 bandwagon that is currently going around with Star Wars. Surely this is the [perfect storm](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_storm) of quizzes.

***Obligatory results:***

37/43. I love Star Wars as much as the next IT professional but [come on](http://www.starwars.com/databank/character/barada/index.html)!
