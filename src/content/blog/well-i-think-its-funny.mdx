Made by the students of the [Columbia Business School](http://www0.gsb.columbia.edu/students/organizations/follies/), this hilarious spoof ["Every Change of Rate"](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3u2qRXb4xCU) features a man lamenting missing out on the Chairmanship of the Federal Reserve to the tune of "Every breath you take". Glenn Hubbard, Dean of the Columbia Business School, was rumored to be on the shortlist for the position which makes the whole thing even better. Here's a sample:

> 
> 
> "First you move your lips
> 
> 
> And hike a few more [BiPS](http://financial-dictionary.thefreedictionary.com/Basis+points).
> 
> 
> When demand then dips And the [yield curve flips](http://www.garynorth.com/public/department81.cfm),
> 
> 
> I'll be watching you."
> 

Brilliant.
