[Chris Auld](http://www.syringe.net.nz/), Intergen director, and [Reed Shaffner](http://blogs.technet.com/reedblog/), Microsoft Office technical product manager, did a presentation at MIX09 showing off [ButtercupReader](http://james.newtonking.com/archive/2009/03/18/buttercupreader-a-silverlight-digital-talking-book-reader.aspx). The mini breakout session was called [Building Accessible RIAs in Microsoft Silverlight](http://sessions.visitmix.com/MIX09/T65M) and focuses on Silverlight’s accessibility features.
